How to play Kamayuu

Kamayuu is a card game built entirely around memory, position tracking and nerve. Every player keeps four cards face down in four fixed positions. At the deal, only your bottom two are revealed to you — the rest you have to remember, or forget at your own risk. Draw, swap, and match discards to lower your hand, then decide when the moment is right to buzz.

On your turn, draw a card and either slot it into one of your four positions, discarding what you removed, or discard the draw outright. You can also take the public discard pile's top card and swap it into a position, unless you were the one who just discarded it. During any player's turn, anyone who is not holding a draw can throw a match: tap a card you believe shares a rank with the top discard. A correct match removes your card without spending a turn. A wrong match reveals your card, returns it, and adds the top discard as a penalty card; if it was your turn, that turn ends. Red Jacks let you peek at one of your own cards. Red Queens let you compare a card of yours against an opponent's and choose whether to trade. When you believe your hand adds up to the lowest total at the table, buzz at the start of your turn and play that turn, or just after your turn before the next player draws. Every other remaining player gets one final turn, then all hands reveal and the lowest total wins.

Frequently asked questions

What is Kamayuu?

Kamayuu is a free online card game of memory, position tracking and buzzing. Each player keeps four cards face down in fixed positions, sees only two of them at the start, and has to remember what's where while drawing, swapping and matching discards.

How do you play Kamayuu?

On your turn, draw from the deck and either replace one of your four cards or discard the draw, or take the top discard and replace a card with it. At any point during another player's turn you can tap a card you think matches the top discard for a free match; guess wrong and it's revealed to everyone. When you believe you hold the lowest total, buzz — every other player gets one final turn, then all hands reveal and the lowest total wins.

Card values and winning

Aces are worth 1; number cards their face value; Jacks 11; Queens 12; black Kings 13. Red Kings are worth zero. Red Jack and Queen powers apply only when drawn from the deck and can be passed. Tied lowest totals share the win. Reaching six cards on your board eliminates you; the last remaining player wins automatically.
