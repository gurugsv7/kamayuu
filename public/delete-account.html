Delete an account or data

Pre-release draft · 4 November 2026. Parent verification is not configured and the local release has not been published. These documents require operator approval before release.

You can request deletion even when you cannot open the app. Email builtbygsv@gmail.com with the subject “Kamayuu account deletion”. Identify your account using its game alias or the email used for your own sign-in. Do not send passwords, child identity documents or private keys. We will verify account ownership before acting.

Older account holders can also use Settings → Delete my online account. A verified parent can use Parent and family controls → Delete child account. Parents may request review or deletion of their child's information by email.

Deletion removes the authentication account, profile, rank and linked gameplay payloads. Other players retain their aggregate statistics. Minimal revoked-consent references prevent reuse of withdrawn approval; processor backups and security-log retention will be specified in the approved release policy. Local settings and solo statistics in the bundled Android app can be removed through Android Settings → Apps → Kamayuu → Storage. Uninstalling removes local app data but does not delete your online account; use the account-deletion options above. Browser-play data is managed separately in your browser.
