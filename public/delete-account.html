01Request deletion by email

Request Kamayuu account deletion

Send to builtbygsv@gmail.com with the subject Kamayuu account deletion. Tell us the game name or sign-in email associated with your own account and whether you want the account and associated data deleted. We may ask you to verify account ownership before acting. Do not send passwords, identity documents, access tokens or private keys.

We aim to respond within 30 days. If verification, a legal obligation or a complex request requires more time, we will explain the next steps. Parents can use the same address to raise a concern about data associated with a child.

02Delete from the game

If you can sign in, open Settings → Delete my online account and confirm the request. Account deletion is permanent. If deletion cannot be completed or you no longer have access to the account, use the email option above.

03What account deletion removes

Deletion removes the authentication account, game profile, account ratings and linked account/gameplay records from the active service. Shared match payloads are removed where required to delete the account’s linked data. Other players may retain their aggregate statistics without your account being restored.

Technical security logs, an active legal/abuse investigation, and any rotating provider backups may have separate retention requirements. They are not used to recreate a deleted account. Where a specific record must be retained for a legal reason, we will explain the applicable exception when responding. See storage and retention.

04Delete local practice data

Under-13 practice does not create an online account. To remove local data, clear the site’s data in your browser, or use Android Settings → Apps → Kamayuu → Storage → Clear storage. This clears local preferences and practice progress. Uninstalling or clearing app storage does not delete an existing online account.

05Google sign-in and guest accounts

Deleting a Kamayuu account does not delete your Google Account. You can separately remove Kamayuu’s connection in your Google Account settings. Guest players who have lost browser access should provide the game name and any available match details so we can investigate ownership; do not submit another person’s account information.
