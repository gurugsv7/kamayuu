Pre-release draft · 4 November 2026. Operator review and public hosting are still required before release.

Local practice data

Under-13 practice has no online account. On Android, clear local settings and solo progress through Settings → Apps → Kamayuu → Storage. Browser practice data can be cleared using the browser’s site-data controls.

Online accounts

Online account holders can use Settings → Delete my online account. You can also email builtbygsv@gmail.com with the subject “Kamayuu account deletion”. Identify your account using its game alias or your sign-in email. Do not send passwords, identity documents or private keys. We verify account ownership before acting. Parents may contact us about data associated with a child.

Deletion removes authentication, profile, rank and linked gameplay payloads. Other players retain aggregate statistics. Processor backup and security-log retention must be specified in the approved release policy. Uninstalling clears local Android data but does not delete an online account.
