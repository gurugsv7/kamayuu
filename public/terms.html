01About the service

BuiltbyGSV operates Kamayuu at kamayuu.builtbygsv.in and through the Kamayuu Android app. These terms govern your use of the game. Please read our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to these terms, do not use the online service.

02Age and eligibility

Practice is available without an online account. Players who select Under 13 are limited to practice on their device. Online accounts, private tables, ranked play and online leaderboards require you to be at least 13 and meet any higher minimum age or guardian-permission requirement imposed by applicable law. An age selection is not a method of obtaining parental consent. Do not give a false age to bypass restrictions.

03Accounts and player identity

Keep your account and device secure. Provide accurate information where required, choose an appropriate game name and do not impersonate someone else. Do not share passwords, sign-in tokens or table access codes publicly. Guest accounts may depend on data stored in your browser; clearing that data can remove your ability to recover the same guest account. Contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

04Fair play and interactions

Do not cheat, exploit bugs, automate gameplay against other players, manipulate rankings, harass others, use hateful or sexual names, collect other players’ personal information, or disrupt the service. Do not attempt to access another player’s hidden cards or backend data. Online messages and reactions are restricted to game-provided presets. Report abuse or security issues to builtbygsv@gmail.com; include relevant game details without sending passwords or unrelated personal information.

We may restrict abusive accounts, correct invalid results or suspend access to protect the service. Contact support if you believe a restriction was applied in error.

05Ranked matches and simulated rivals

Where ranked play is available, matchmaking prioritizes human opponents and may use AI-controlled rivals to fill gaps. Both kinds of match may affect your rating, and leaderboards may include AI-controlled rivals. Fictional rival names, avatars, reactions and varied timing do not establish that a rival is a human. The ranked entry notice explains this before participation.

Ratings and results are in-game statistics, not money or a guarantee of skill or rewards. We may adjust matchmaking, rating rules and seasons, and investigate manipulated results. Features shown as unavailable are not a promise of an opening date.
