Privacy information

Pre-release draft · 4 November 2026. Parent verification is not configured and the local release has not been published. These documents require operator approval before release.

What the game uses

Age group is stored on your device; we do not ask for your exact birthday. Solo play does not require an online account. Online play uses Supabase authentication, a game profile, game actions, match results and rating. Older users may choose Google sign-in or guest sign-in protected by CAPTCHA. Hosting and security services may process IP addresses and technical request logs.

Children

Under-13 online play requires verified parental consent first. A child gets a generated game alias and an internal account identifier, without supplying a real name, email, photo or school. We store the consent receipt reference, parent reference and approved feature settings. The parent verification service separately handles adult verification data according to its notice. This service has not yet been selected.

Product engagement analytics and Google/CAPTCHA sign-in are disabled for child users. Essential game state, rank and match counts are still needed to run online play. Online profiles and rankings are visible to other players using game aliases. Children see pseudonyms in place of other players' freeform names. Communication is limited to preset reactions. No advertising or purchases are included in this candidate.

Parent choices and deletion

Parents can manage online access, preset reactions and rematches through verified parent controls, withdraw consent, and request deletion. See parent information and deletion options. Gameplay/account records are removed through deletion; minimal revoked-consent references are retained to prevent reuse of withdrawn approval. Exact retention periods, verification provider details and backup/log handling must be finalized before release.

Older players

Account-linked session and gameplay events help us understand game usage. Google and CAPTCHA services receive information when their respective sign-in features are used. We do not sell personal information.
