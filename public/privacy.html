How Kamayuu handles information when you practise, sign in and play online.

01Who operates Kamayuu

Kamayuu is operated by BuiltbyGSV in India. This policy covers kamayuu.builtbygsv.in and our Kamayuu Android app. For privacy questions and requests, email builtbygsv@gmail.com.

02Information we use

  • On your device: your selected age group, preferences, practice progress and sign-in session where you choose to sign in. We ask for an age group, not an exact birthday.
  • Account information: an account identifier, chosen display name and game avatar; Google sign-in may provide your Google identifier, email address, name and profile picture to our authentication service. Your email address is not shown on the game leaderboard.
  • Online gameplay: table membership, actions, preset reactions, match results, ratings and account statistics needed to run and protect the game.
  • Usage and security information: account-linked session and gameplay events for eligible online users, plus technical request information such as IP addresses and service logs processed by our hosting and security providers.
  • Support: information you choose to send when contacting us. Do not send passwords, government IDs or private signing keys.

03Why we use it

We use information to authenticate players, save profiles, operate multiplayer tables and rankings, reconnect interrupted games, prevent abuse, handle support and deletion requests, and understand game usage. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar. Google sign-in is used for identity and account access, not to collect unrelated Google data.

04Children and age choices

In this version, players who select Under 13 receive practice on their device only. They cannot sign in, join private tables or ranked matches, or use online leaderboards. We do not offer parent verification or child online account creation in this release. Google/CAPTCHA sign-in and product engagement analytics are disabled for these players.

Practice progress and settings stay on the device. Downloading the website or app and using support can still involve technical processing by hosting or security services. A self-declared age group is not verified proof of age. A higher minimum age or guardian permission may be required by applicable local law. If you believe a child has supplied online account data, contact us so we can investigate and handle the request.

05Service providers and visibility

We use Supabase for authentication, database and multiplayer services, and Vercel for website hosting. Google processes Google sign-in; browser guest sign-in may use hCaptcha for abuse prevention. Our Android app uses Android platform services, including Credential Manager and Keystore, for sign-in and protected local session storage. Providers may process data in countries outside your own.

Other players can see your game display name, avatar, rankings and relevant match information. Online communication is limited to preset reactions and messages. AI-controlled rivals may appear in ranked play where that feature is available. We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising or provide it to advertisers. We may disclose information where necessary to meet legal obligations or investigate abuse.

06Storage, security and retention

We use HTTPS, authenticated server endpoints and access controls for online data. Native Android sign-in sessions use encrypted storage backed by Android Keystore; browser sessions and preferences use browser storage. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Account profiles, statistics and linked gameplay records are retained while your account exists and to operate the associated game features, unless you request deletion. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request. Hosting/security logs and any rotating backups follow the applicable provider settings; deletion from the active game does not mean a rotating backup disappears immediately. Information required for a specific legal obligation or abuse investigation may be retained for that purpose rather than routine gameplay. We do not use retained backup copies to recreate a deleted account.

07Your choices and deletion

You can practise without an online account, sign out, change available preferences, and request access, correction or deletion of your account data. Use Settings → Delete my online account, or follow the web deletion instructions without reinstalling the app. We verify ownership before disclosing or deleting account data. Contact us if you need help exercising rights available under your local law.

Clearing browser site data or Android app storage removes local preferences and practice data. Uninstalling does not delete an online account. You can separately manage Kamayuu’s Google connection in your Google Account.

08Changes and contact

We will update this page when these practices change and update the date above. Material changes may also be explained in the game. Contact builtbygsv@gmail.com with “Kamayuu privacy” in the subject.
