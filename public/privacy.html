Privacy information

Pre-release draft · 4 November 2026. Operator review and public hosting are still required before release.

Practice and age choice

Age group, settings and solo progress are stored on your device. We do not ask for an exact birthday. Practice does not require an online account.

Under-13 players

This release provides local practice only for players who select Under 13. They cannot sign in, join ranked matches or private tables, use online leaderboards or create child online accounts. There is no parent verification or KWS integration in this release. Google/CAPTCHA sign-in and product engagement analytics are disabled for these players.

Online players

For ages 13 and above, online play uses Supabase authentication, a game profile, game actions, match results and rating. Native Android uses Google sign-in; browser players may also use CAPTCHA-protected guest sign-in. Online profiles and rankings are visible to other players. Account-linked session and gameplay events help us understand usage. Google and CAPTCHA services receive information when their sign-in features are used. We do not sell personal information. No advertising or purchases are included in this candidate.

Technical data and deletion

Hosting and security services may process IP addresses and technical request logs, including when downloading the game. Local-only play is not a promise that initial downloads involve no network processing. See deletion options. Exact retention periods, processor backup/log handling and India age treatment must be reviewed before public release.
