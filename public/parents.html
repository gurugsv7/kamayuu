01Practice without an account

Kamayuu is a memory card game. Players can learn the rules and practise against computer-controlled opponents without signing in. Under-13 players go straight to this local practice experience after one age-group question. We do not ask for an exact birthday.

02What is unavailable to under-13 players

Under-13 players cannot use Google or guest sign-in, join ranked or private online tables, use online leaderboards, or create online child accounts. Parent approval does not unlock these features in this version. There is no KWS or other parent-verification flow to complete.

Online access for other age groups is subject to the eligibility rules, including any higher minimum age or guardian-permission requirement under applicable local law. The age choice is self-declared; please help your child select it accurately.

03Data and interactions

For under-13 practice, progress and preferences stay on the device. Product engagement analytics and Google/CAPTCHA sign-in are disabled. Downloading the game or contacting support can still involve hosting/security request information. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Online players use game profiles and preset messages/reactions. Ranked play, when available, can include AI-controlled rivals as explained before entry. Encourage children not to share real names, school details, addresses or contact information with other players.

04Removing data or raising a concern

Clear local practice data through browser site-data controls or Android Settings → Apps → Kamayuu → Storage. If you believe a child supplied online account information, email builtbygsv@gmail.com. We can investigate and handle access or deletion requests after appropriate verification. Do not email identity documents or passwords. See deletion instructions.
