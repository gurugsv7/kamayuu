Kamayuu

Information for parents

Pre-release draft · 4 November 2026. Operator review and public hosting are still required before release.

Kamayuu is a memory card game with practice on this device and online matches for ages 13 and above in this release.

Under-13 players can enjoy practice without an online account. Ranked matches, private tables, online leaderboards, account sign-in and parent-verification setup are unavailable to them. We ask one age-group question, not an exact birthday. An age choice is not verified proof of age.

Under-13 practice progress and settings stay on the device. Google/CAPTCHA sign-in and product engagement analytics are disabled for these players. Initial downloads and hosting security logs may still involve technical request information.

Online ranked matchmaking prioritizes real players and may use AI-controlled opponents. Both count toward rank. Online communication uses preset reactions.
