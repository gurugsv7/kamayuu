Kamayuu

Information for parents

Pre-release draft · 4 November 2026. Parent verification is not configured and the local release has not been published. These documents require operator approval before release.

Kamayuu is a memory card game with solo practice and online matches. Ranked play prioritizes real players and may use AI-controlled opponents; both count toward rank and the leaderboard includes AI-controlled rivals.

Before a child plays online, a parent must read the privacy information and complete verification. Approval covers account creation, essential gameplay processing, generated profile visibility and ranking. Preset reactions and rematches are separately controllable. Approval for optional interactions is not needed for solo play or basic online gameplay.

Children should never share their real name, address, school or contact information with players. The game offers preset reactions instead of freeform chat. Parents can disable online access or interactions in Settings → Parent and family controls. Only a verified parent can manage a child account; a child's age selection is not parental consent.

Verification is not operational in this local candidate. Provider details, parent authentication and retention terms must be approved before online child access is released.
